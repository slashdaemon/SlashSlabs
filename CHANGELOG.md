# Changelog

All notable changes to SlashSlabs. Versions follow `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`; each release ships one
jar per Minecraft line (`slashslabs-<version>+mc<mc>-fabric.jar`).

## 0.2.0

For Minecraft **26.1.2, 26.2 and 26.3**, as before.

### Added

- **Red sand slab.** Red sand rises get a real red sand slab instead of smooth red sandstone.
- **Seven terracotta slabs** in the badlands colours: plain, orange, yellow, brown, red, white and
  light gray. Badlands terracotta rises are now smoothed (vanilla has no terracotta slab, so they
  were left as full-block steps). Terracotta slabs:
  - are bottom-only: clicking the upper half of a block still places a bottom slab;
  - need a pickaxe to drop, like terracotta.
- Recipes for every new slab: 3 blocks → 6 slabs, 2 slabs → 1 block, and stonecutter 1 → 2.

### Changed

- **Sand rises always get a real sand slab**, which looks and sounds like sand. Smooth sandstone
  looked flat, and it broke and stepped like stone.
- **Bottom slabs are sent to clients as sculk sensor states** with an unused redstone power level.
  Polymer has 150 of these, so bottom slabs are no longer limited to four materials. Top slabs
  still use the four waxed copper states: dirt, sand, red sand and grass. Top grass slabs show the
  first palette green in every biome. This is invisible with the resource pack. Without it, bottom
  slabs appear as sculk sensors instead of copper slabs.
- Sculk sensor step, hit and fall sounds are now played by the server, as copper's already were,
  so slabs sound like their real material.
- The self-test grew from about 400 to about 730 checks. They cover every material, bottom-only
  placement and tool rules, and the test field now has red sand and terracotta strips.

### Removed

- The `sandSlab` option. Sand always gets a real slab now, and it no longer costs a grass green.
  An old config keeps the key harmlessly until the file is rewritten.

### Known effects

- **Faint glow.** Sculk sensors emit light level 1 on the client. After a slab changes in view,
  the client may show a barely visible glow next to it until the chunk reloads.

### Upgrading from 0.1.0

- Every slab moves to a different borrowed state, so clients' **Voxy LODs** of explored terrain
  show the old look until Voxy rebuilds them.
- Terrain generated by 0.1.0 keeps its smooth sandstone and smooth red sandstone steps. To convert
  an area, run `/slashslabs purge <radius>` and then `/slashslabs smooth <radius>`.

## 0.1.0

First release, for Minecraft **26.1.2, 26.2 and 26.3** (Fabric; Fabric API required; Fabric
Loader 0.18.4 or newer).

### Added

- **Terrain smoothing at world generation.** A half-slab step at every one-block rise, with
  material from the lower column:
  - grass → grass slab;
  - dirt family → dirt slab;
  - sand → smooth sandstone slab;
  - stone family → matching vanilla slab;
  - snowy biomes → 5 snow layers;
  - shallow water → waterlogged slab.

  Cliffs, structures, flowers, saplings, deep water and lava edges are skipped. Smoothing runs at
  each chunk's LIGHT step, so the result doesn't depend on generation order; it costs about 1 ms
  per chunk.
- **Grass slab and dirt slab blocks** (plus an optional sand slab) that vanilla clients see
  without any mod, through Polymer:
  - grass spreads, decays and gets pulled onto dirt slabs;
  - Silk Touch drops;
  - recipes and stonecutting;
  - two slabs make the vanilla full block;
  - mining runs server-side at grass/dirt speed;
  - correct break and footstep sounds.
- **Three grass greens**, chosen per block from the biome colour; default palette fitted to a
  67,000-sample terrain survey.
- **Server resource pack** generated at startup from the vanilla client jar (the mod jar contains
  no Minecraft textures) and served on the game port by Polymer AutoHost.
- **Commands** under `/slashslabs`: `info`, `smooth` (retrofit existing terrain), `purge`
  (removal), `survey` (terrain statistics and palette fitting), `test-field`, `selftest`, plus
  diagnostics. Op level 2, or `slashslabs.command.*` nodes with LuckPerms or another
  fabric-permissions-api provider.
- **Optional step-height module** (step up full blocks; off while sneaking; skipped for Bedrock
  players) and optional animal spawning on grass slabs.
- **BlueMap support** through models inside the jar.

### Known limitations

See [README → Limitations](README.md#limitations): four shared slab slots, three fixed greens,
server-played copper sounds, the resource pack is required for correct visuals, and Bedrock
players see copper slabs.
